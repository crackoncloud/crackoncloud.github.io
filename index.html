About

crackoncloud is a small independent studio building apps for Android and iOS — media players, an e-reader and a screen time tool. The apps are free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade.

Your files, your storage

Folders on the device and drives you already pay for. Connect an account once and browse it like local storage.

Works offline

No connection, no problem. Local files open with the full feature set, nothing gated behind a network.

Yours to shape

Themes, equalizers, subtitles, fonts, playback speed, blocking rules — the details are settings, not opinions.
