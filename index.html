Shadow Reader

E-reader with read-aloud · Android

Every book you own, read or listened to. EPUB, PDF, comics and office documents from folders you already have, with a voice that follows the words and your place kept on every device.

  • EPUB, PDF, CBZ, MOBI, FB2, DOCX, ODT and more
  • Sentence-synced read-aloud, free for everybody
  • Background playback and sleep timer
  • Highlights, notes and bookmarks that export
  • Fonts, themes, e-ink mode and gestures
  • OPDS catalogues and watched folders
  • Cloud sources and sync through your own storage (Pro)
  • No account, no ads, one-time Pro unlock

Ruk

App blocker & screen time · Android

A few deliberate seconds between the impulse and the feed. Ruk pauses the apps that take your day, holds them to a daily limit or a schedule, and shows you afterwards what you took back.

  • A breathing pause before an app opens
  • Daily screen time limits, per app or per group
  • Schedules, including across midnight
  • Focus sessions with an allow list
  • Feed blocking (experimental)
  • Strict mode and the attention budget
  • Real screen time insights and export
  • No account, no ads, nothing sent anywhere

  • 5-band equalizer with presets
  • Embedded lyrics and visualizers

  • Sleep timer, shuffle, repeat, quick search

  • Adjustable playback speed (Pro)

About

crackoncloud is a small independent studio building apps for Android and iOS — media players, an e-reader and a screen time tool. The apps are free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade.

Your files, your storage

Folders on the device and drives you already pay for. Connect an account once and browse it like local storage.

Works offline

No connection, no problem. Local files open with the full feature set, nothing gated behind a network.

Yours to shape

Themes, equalizers, subtitles, fonts, playback speed, blocking rules — the details are settings, not opinions.
