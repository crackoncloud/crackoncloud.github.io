Spiral Player

Cloud & offline music player · Android and iOS

A music player for the library you already have. Play local files or stream from your cloud drives, with lossless audio, a real equalizer and themes you can bend to taste.

  • Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud
  • FLAC and lossless audio support
  • 5-band equalizer with presets
  • Embedded lyrics and visualizers
  • Unlimited theme customization
  • Sleep timer, shuffle, repeat, quick search
  • Backup and restore
  • Adjustable playback speed (Pro)

Vot Player

Cloud & offline video player · Android

Play any video, anywhere it lives. Stream from your cloud drives or open local files offline, with subtitle control, picture-in-picture and an equalizer with volume boost.

  • Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, pCloud
  • Offline playback of popular formats
  • Custom subtitles with styling controls
  • Picture-in-picture and background play

  • Playlists, bookmarks and blacklist
  • Light, dark and night themes
  • Adjustable playback speed (Pro)

About

crackoncloud is a small independent studio building media apps for Android and iOS. The apps are free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade.

Cloud-first

Your drives are just another folder. Connect an account once and browse it like local storage.

Works offline

No connection, no problem. Local files play with the full feature set, nothing gated behind a network.

Yours to shape

Themes, equalizers, subtitles, playback speed — the details are settings, not opinions.
