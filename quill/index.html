Eight word puzzles. Made fresh on your phone.

The eight

One desk, eight kinds of puzzle, and every one of them free to play. Each has its own board, its own rules sheet of four lines, and its own bests.

SearchFind every word on the list, in any direction, on boards from 8×8 to 16×16. Extra real words go in the jar.
ScrambleDrag through the letter wheel. The words that fit drop into a crossword; fill every slot.
LadderTurn one word into another, one letter a rung. Every rung must be a word, and any valid path counts.
HiveSeven letters, one in the centre, every word you can make of four or more. Use all seven for a pangram.
GridSwipe through touching tiles. Every real word counts, and there is a timed mode if you want one.
Fill-InEvery word on the list fits the grid once. Drag a word onto a slot of its length; the crossings must agree.
CipherA public-domain quotation in a substitution cipher. Tap a letter, type its real one, decode the whole line.
FiveGuess the hidden word in six tries, with a hard mode, and longer words behind the unlock.

Made, not served

Quill has no level packs to run out of. Each puzzle is generated on your phone the moment you open it, from a tiered English lexicon — common words for the answers, a wide dictionary for what you find along the way.

  • Four levels, Easy to Expert, and the level is graded before you see the board, so Easy is easy and Expert is not.
  • The lexicon is tiered by how common a word is: the answers come from words people use, and the wide dictionary only decides what else counts.
  • A word search mixes its directions and crosses its words, and every grid is a fresh one.
  • The same seed gives the same puzzle on every device, which is what lets the daily be built from the date rather than downloaded.
  • A hundred and fifty themed word search packs come along too, each generated fresh every time it is opened.

A Post every day

Every day brings a Post of eight, one puzzle of each kind, built from the date on your device and the same for everyone. Nothing is fetched, and Monday's is the same in every time zone.

  • Solve any one of the eight and your streak lives. Solve all eight and you bank a freeze for a day you miss, up to three.
  • A freeze is spent for you, automatically, on the day it is needed. There is no way to buy one.
  • The streak is counted by the calendar, not the clock, so a day can only ever count once.
  • Today's Post is free. The calendar keeps every day, and the archive of past days opens with the unlock.

Nothing you can lose

No ads. No energy, no lives, no coins, and no timer on a puzzle unless you ask for one. Nothing in this game ends a puzzle for you.

Check, Undo, Restart, Shuffle

All unlimited and all free. Restart asks first, and you can turn even that off.

A hint is a button you press

A small bank that refills with time, and nothing is sold by the piece. Nudge makes the hint glow when you have been stuck a while; that can be turned off too.

The clock is yours

Every board keeps a time for your bests, and you can hide it. The only timer that ends anything is the Grid's Timed mode, and you chose it.

Pick up where you stopped

An open puzzle is kept move by move, and the desk offers it back to you. Leave a Cipher half done for a week and it is still half done.

Two looks

Glass, the default: panes over calm, living scenes, in five accents. Study: a rendered desk of oak, ceramic, slate, brass and marble on paper, in four rooms. Switch whenever you like, in Settings.

  • Glass has Day, Dusk, Contrast and Midnight, five accents from Ocean to Lilac, a scene or a plain background, and a Solid surfaces switch for anyone who cannot read through a pane.
  • The Study has the Studio, the Lamp, the Slate and the Ink, and tiles of oak, ceramic, slate, brass and marble.
  • Ambient motion is Full, Calm or Off, and Reduce motion cuts instead of moving.
  • Three text sizes, large targets, colour-blind marks that draw a shape rather than only a colour, and a left-handed layout.
  • A screen reader reads the board tile by tile, and the app speaks 22 languages.

Inside the app

Screens off a real device, nothing redrawn.

Yours, on your phone

No account, no sign-in, no server. Every puzzle is made on the device, and your progress is written to a database on it. Nothing about your play leaves the phone unless you switch something on.

Offline

The lexicon, the generators, the hints and the save file are all on the device, and so is the daily. Airplane mode changes nothing but the store.

A backup that is a file you keep

Settings › Data › Export backup writes one file, wherever you choose. Import it on another device and it merges, never deletes. The app has no way to send it anywhere.

One switch, yours

Send diagnostics, in Settings › Data: crash reports and which puzzles are hard. No ad id, no user id. It starts switched off in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.

The whole account is in the privacy policy.

Questions

Where do the puzzles come from?

From your phone. A generator builds each one from the bundled lexicon when you open it, and grades it against the level you asked for before it is drawn. The daily Post is seeded from the date, so every device builds the same eight with nothing downloaded. The 150 word search packs are lists and a picture; the board is generated fresh every time you open one.

What counts as a word?

A tiered English lexicon. Answers come from the common tier, so a Hive or a Ladder never rests on a word nobody uses; the wide dictionary decides what else you are allowed to find along the way. Slurs and obscenities are kept out of every grid, forwards and reversed. You can choose US or UK spelling in Settings › Play.

How does the streak work?

Solving any one of today's eight keeps it alive. Solving all eight banks a freeze, up to three, and a freeze is spent automatically on a day you miss. Only today's Post moves the streak: a past day solved from the calendar is history, and moving the device clock does nothing.

Is there a move limit or a timer?

No move limit on any type, and no timer unless you pick the Grid's Timed mode. Every board keeps a time for your bests, and Settings › Play › Show clock hides it. Check, Undo, Restart and Shuffle are unlimited.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. The only things that ever want a connection are the store — the purchase, the restore, the update check and the rating sheet — and the optional diagnostics switch.

Do I need an account?

There isn't one. No sign-in, no profile, no leaderboard, and no server of ours for any of them to live on. To move to a new phone, export a backup from Settings › Data and import it there.

What does the unlock cost, and is it a subscription?

It is one purchase, once, priced by the store in your own currency, and it is not a subscription. It adds every size and level, the Post archive, all the packs, the fourth room and two tile faces, all-time statistics and a faster hint bank. No type is behind it.

Which platforms is it on?

Android 8.0 and up and iOS 15 and up, built from one source set. Both are in preparation for their stores, and this page is where each listing will be announced.
