A partner who plays like one

One AI for every seat, at four levels you set per seat, so you can have a strong partner and gentle opponents, or the other way round. It searches thousands of possible deals before every card, then chooses the conventional play among the good ones.

  • Second hand low, third hand high. Cover the nil. Lead trump as the maker. Never over-trump a partner's winner. The conventions are tested by name, and a failure is a bug rather than a tuning item.
  • Easy, Standard, Strong and Expert are search budgets, counted in iterations rather than milliseconds, so the same level plays the same on every phone.
  • Ask the Coach and it shows the expert's card, the reason in one sentence, and how the other candidates rated. Three a match in Play, unlimited in Practice; the unlock takes the limit off.
  • A hand review holds every trick of every hand, and with the unlock any trick row can show what Expert would have played instead.
  • A Spades bid comes from rolling the hand out and choosing by expected score, calibrated so Expert takes what it bids, give or take a bag.

The computer never sees your cards

The second complaint in every card app's reviews is answered here with evidence rather than a denial.

  • Before a hand is dealt, its shuffle is committed to a code, and six characters of it sit on the table for the whole hand.
  • When the hand is over, the review shows the seed and lets you check that it makes the same code — in the app, or with any SHA-256 tool on any computer.
  • The opponents' search is handed only what a player at the table could see. The type that carries it cannot hold a hidden card, and the app's own test mutates the hidden cards five hundred times per game and checks the decision does not change.

A Daily, a Tour, and a table for the family

Four ways to sit down that are not another match against the same four seats.

The Daily

One game is featured each day, in turn. You play four hands from the day's seed; the expert plays the same four; your score is the margin between you. Solve it and your streak grows; every seventh day banks a freeze, up to two, spent for you on a day you miss.

The Tour

Forty chapters of six matches, 240 in all, that introduce one rule at a time and climb from Easy tables to Expert ones, with stars for the margin. Every chapter is verified winnable by an Expert seat. The first three chapters are free.

Pass & Play

Two, three or four people at one phone. The table turns to whoever is holding it, and the hand-off screen hides the cards in between.

Practice

Any game, any rules, no score kept, and the Coach without limit — including its bid, marked on the sheet.

Your table

Five tables and five decks, drawn for a phone rather than scanned from a box. Cards deal on springs, a lifted card parts its neighbours, and a won trick gathers under the winner's badge.

  • Graphite, Paper and Midnight tables and the Studio, Bold and Classic decks are free; Velvet and Copper, Ink and Neon come with the unlock.
  • Four-colour suits, local court letters in every language, three card sizes, a left-handed layout, sort by suit or by rank.
  • Play a card with two taps, one tap, or a drag. Confirm point cards, auto-play the only legal card, undo, set the thinking delay and the trick pause.
  • Animation at Normal, Fast or Off; Reduce motion cuts instead of moving. Sound and haptics each have a switch.
  • A screen reader can play at the table — every card is spoken by name — and the app speaks 22 languages.

No account, no sign-in, no server. Every deal is shuffled on the device, every card is chosen on it, and every match, hand and result is written to a database on it. Your statistics are never deleted.

Questions

How do I know the computer isn't cheating?

Two ways. Before a hand is dealt, its shuffle is committed to a SHA-256 code shown on the table; after the hand, the review shows the seed and you can check that it makes the same code, in the app or on any computer. And the opponents' search is given only what a seated player could see: the type that carries it has no field for a hidden card, and a test mutates the hidden cards five hundred times per game and checks the decision does not change. A failure of that test blocks a release.

What are the levels, and why is Expert sometimes "reduced"?

Easy, Standard, Strong and Expert are search budgets, counted in iterations so a level plays the same on every phone. On a slow phone Expert can take a few seconds a card; after three slow plays the app runs Expert at half its search and says so in Settings › About. The reduced Expert still beats Standard, which is why it keeps the name.

How does the Daily work?

One game is featured each day, in turn. You play four hands from the day's seed and the expert plays the same four; your score is the margin between you, so a bad deal is bad for both of you. Any device builds the same four hands from the date, nothing is downloaded, and today's is free. The other three games on a given day, and past days, open with the unlock. Only today moves the streak; a past day played from the calendar is history.

Can I play with people in the room?

Yes: Pass & Play seats two, three or four people at one phone, with the AI filling the rest. The table turns to whoever is holding the phone, and the hand-off screen hides the cards until the next person taps and holds. There is no online play and no leaderboard at 1.0.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. The only things that ever want a connection are the store — the purchase, the restore, Android's update check and the rating sheet — and the two diagnostics switches, if you have them on.

Do I need an account?

There isn't one. No sign-in, no profile, and no server of ours for either to live on. To move to a new phone, export a backup from Settings › Backup and import it there; the import merges, so nothing you have is ever lowered by it.

What does the unlock cost, and is it a subscription?

It is one purchase, once, priced by the store in your own currency, and it is not a subscription. It adds the Strong and Expert opponents, every house rule, the whole Tour, every past Daily, the Coach without limit, and two tables and two decks. No game is behind it.
