The logging screen is the app

Everything else is downstream of how fast a set goes in between two working sets.

  • A numeric keypad, never a scroll wheel — and the row is prefilled with what you lifted last time, not what a template hoped for.
  • A rest timer that runs with the screen off and survives the app being killed. Rest per set, shorter rest after warm-ups, superset rest that waits for the block.
  • A set timer for planks and carries, and intervals for EMOM, Tabata, AMRAP and circuits.
  • Warm-up sets generated from your working weight, and plate maths for the bar in front of you.
  • Machine taken? Swap in an alternative for the same muscle with the equipment you have.

  • Seven ways to track a movement — weight and reps, assisted bodyweight, duration and the rest.

Routines, and real programs behind them

Questions

Do I have to make an account?

No, and there is nothing to make one with. AtomFit has no user accounts and no server. The only sign-in anywhere in the app is an optional Google sign-in, and it exists solely to reach the private folder in your own Drive that sync uses.

How does sync work with no backend?

Each device writes its changes into the appDataFolder of your own Google Drive — a hidden space only this app can read — and reads the other devices' changes back out. The merge happens on the phone. We never see the file, and deleting it in your Drive ends the whole thing without asking us.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. The database is on the device, and every screen reads from it. Sync is the only feature that ever wants a connection, and it waits until there is one.

Is Pro a subscription?

No — one payment through Google Play, bought once and restored on your next phone. The entitlement is cached on the device, so it keeps working with no connection.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. AtomFit imports Strong, Hevy, FitNotes and Jefit exports, and shows you a preview with an integrity check before anything is written.

Can I get my data back out?

Full-fidelity JSON export in one tap, with no account and no upgrade. A session or a month can also leave as PDF, CSV or TCX, and automatic rolling backups go to a folder you choose.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not today. AtomFit is Android, with a Wear OS watch app alongside it.
